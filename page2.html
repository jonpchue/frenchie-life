Where French Bulldogs Came From

Even though French Bulldogs have France in their name, their history actually started in England. During the 1800s, small bulldogs were popular with lace workers in Nottingham. Some of these workers moved to France and brought their dogs with them.

The small bulldogs became popular in France. Over time, they developed into the French Bulldog breed we know today. Their bat-like ears became one of their most recognizable features.

Early French Bulldogs

  • They had ancestors in England.
  • They became popular in France.
  • They were bred mainly as companion dogs.

How Frenchies Became Popular

French Bulldogs became fashionable in Paris during the late 1800s. Their small size and friendly personalities made them popular companion dogs.

Eventually, French Bulldogs became popular in other countries, including the United States. Today, they are recognized for their unique appearance and playful personalities.
