Welcome to the Frenchie Life!

French Bulldogs, also known as Frenchies, are small dogs that are known for their big bat-like ears, short noses, and wild personalities.

French Bulldogs can sleep for up to 12 to 14 hours a day. They treat lounging like a full-time job and are champions at matching their owner's low-energy lifestyle. Because of their short, flat snouts, their normal breathing sounds like a tiny engine. They don't just snore while asleep; they also snort, grunt, and wheeze while wide awake. While they are generally lazy, they get sudden, random bursts of frantic energy called FRAPs (Frenetic Random Activity Periods), or "the zoomies." They will sprint in circles for two minutes straight and then immediately crash back to sleep.
